STUDENT VIBE-CODES THE FUTURE WITH AI!

By The Tech Correspondent

It was reported today that Suraj Mondal, a BCA student studying at MAKAUT, has been actively utilizing artificial intelligence to rapidly construct mobile applications and ship hackathon projects at unprecedented speeds.

Unlike traditional developers who write every line manually, Mr. Mondal describes his process as "vibe-coding"—a modern synthesis of logic and AI-assisted prompt engineering. Using advanced systems like Claude and Gemini, he turns conceptual ideas into working products.

"My ultimate goal," Mondal stated to the press, "is to engineer immersive digital virtual reality worlds, akin to the fictional universe seen in Sword Art Online." Authorities confirm he is highly proficient in React Native, Python, and JavaScript.
